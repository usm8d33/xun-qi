Xun-Qi User Guide
=================
(A receiver and organizer compatible with the LocalSend protocol. Xun-Qi is not an official LocalSend product.)
中文說明請看「使用說明.txt」。

[What it is]
Send files from your phone (iPhone or Android) to your PC with the official LocalSend app. Your phone sees two devices:
  - My PC - Send: files are saved straight into "files folder\Sent"
  - My PC - Send & Sort: after saving, EmbeddingGemma 2 sorts them into "files folder\Sorted\category\year-month"
    and adds them to the search index

[Language]
- The interface is available in English and Traditional Chinese (繁體中文). On first start, Xun-Qi follows your
  Windows display language (Chinese -> Traditional Chinese, anything else -> English; if you're upgrading from an
  earlier version, it stays in Traditional Chinese). You can switch at any time in the
  web page under Tools > Language; the page changes right away. The text in the black console window and the device
  names your phone sees (My PC - Send / 我的電腦-傳送) update the next time you start the app. setup.bat,
  download_model.bat and start.bat follow the same setting.
- Switching languages never renames or moves folders, and existing category names stay the same; only the interface
  text changes. Folder names inside the files folder are decided when it's first created: created in English, you get
  "Desktop\Xun-Qi" with "Sent, Sorted, To delete..." and English categories (Food, Scenery...); created in Chinese, you
  get "Xun-Qi 尋棲" with Chinese names. They stay that way afterward.
- The setting is saved in user_settings.json in the app folder ("lang": "en" or "zh").

[Two folders]
- App folder (this folder): only the program, portable Python and the AI models. You can delete and reinstall it
  without touching your files.
- Files folder: "Desktop\Xun-Qi" by default, created on first start.
  (If "Desktop\Xun-Qi 尋棲" or the older "Desktop\LocalSend 收到的檔案" already exists, it's used as is; nothing is moved.)
  Inside:
    Sent\                     files received with "Send"
    Sorted\category\year-month\   results of "Send & Sort"
    Duplicates\  Other files\
    To delete\                files you chose not to keep in Duplicates (Xun-Qi never deletes them; you do)
    Index backups\            backups made in Tools > Backup & Restore
    .xunqi-data\              (hidden) search index, device certificates and logs. Please don't delete it.
  To change the location: Tools > Files Location in the sidebar. Click "Choose..." or paste a path, save, then restart.
  Changing the location doesn't move any files. To keep your existing files: quit the app, move the whole folder in
  File Explorer, then choose it in Tools > Files Location. Categories, index and review status all carry over.
  If your Desktop is synced by OneDrive, received photos will be uploaded to the cloud. Xun-Qi warns you about this;
  choose another location if you don't want that.

[First-time setup (once)]
1. Put the whole app folder where you like (for example D:\XunQi). Keep the path short.
2. Double-click setup.bat. It downloads portable Python and the packages it needs, and asks you to choose an edition
   (with a suggestion based on your graphics card).
   All three editions have the same features; they differ in accuracy, speed and size:
     - 1 Lite: no PyTorch. The AI uses an ONNX int8 model (about 0.5 GB) and DirectML on any graphics card
               (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel), or the CPU. Text recognition uses the mobile model; videos get a frame every
               6 seconds. Smallest, slightly less accurate sorting.
     - 2 Standard: PyTorch + the original EmbeddingGemma 2 model (about 1.5 GB). Mobile text recognition model; a video
               frame every 4 seconds.
     - 3 Flagship: the same AI as Standard, with the server text recognition model (most accurate) and a video frame
               every 2 seconds. For PCs with a dedicated graphics card.
   Standard and Flagship detect your graphics card and pick a PyTorch build:
     - NVIDIA RTX 50 series -> CUDA 13.0 build (about 1.9 GB; needs driver 580 or later, otherwise the CUDA 12.8 build)
     - NVIDIA GTX 16, RTX 20/30/40 series -> CUDA 12.6 build (about 2.6 GB)
     - NVIDIA GTX 10 series or older -> CUDA 12.6 build, pinned to PyTorch 2.14
     - Intel Arc graphics / Core Ultra integrated graphics -> Intel build (about 0.6 GB)
     - AMD graphics or no dedicated graphics card -> CPU build (about 0.1 GB; Lite is recommended for these PCs)
   You can also choose in Command Prompt: setup.bat lite (or standard, flagship). For Standard and Flagship you can add
   a build, for example setup.bat standard cu126. To switch editions or after changing graphics cards, run setup.bat
   again; received files and the index are not affected.
3. Double-click download_model.bat to download the models for your edition:
   - Lite: the int8 model from onnx-community/embeddinggemma-2-ONNX (about 0.5 GB) -> models\embeddinggemma-2-onnx
   - Standard / Flagship: google/embeddinggemma-2 (about 1.5 GB) -> models\embeddinggemma-2 (if you downloaded it
     yourself, put it here; it must include model.safetensors)
4. The OCR models (for reading text in photos) are also downloaded by download_model.bat into models\ocr\:
   Flagship uses server (about 170 MB); Lite and Standard use mobile (about 21 MB). If both are there, the one for your
   edition is preferred. Without them everything else still works; you just can't search text in photos.

[Every time]
1. Quit the LocalSend desktop app completely first (including its icon in the taskbar corner); otherwise it takes the
   same port.
2. Double-click start.bat. The black window shows an address (http://127.0.0.1:8765). Copy it into your browser to
   open the app.
3. The first time, Windows Firewall asks for permission. Check "Private networks" and allow it.
4. On your phone, open LocalSend -> Send -> pick files -> tap "My PC - Send" or "My PC - Send & Sort".
   The phone and the PC must be on the same Wi-Fi.
5. When you're done, just close the black window. Xun-Qi doesn't start automatically with Windows.

[Web interface]
- Home: search box; counts for To Review, Suggested Review, Duplicates and To Delete (click to go there); the latest
  batch; AI and text recognition status; storage used; and a cover photo for each category.
- Sidebar: Library, Categories and Tools can each be expanded or collapsed by clicking the heading, and the page
  remembers your choice. The sidebar scrolls on its own. Click a category to see only that category; click again to
  clear it.
- Each transfer from your phone is a batch. At the top of each batch, "Suggested Review" lists items the AI isn't sure
  about (low confidence, two close categories, Unsorted, or a possible duplicate), each with the reason. If it's wrong,
  use "Change Category"; if it's right, click "Category Is Correct" and it moves down to "Others".
  (These buttons appear when you hover over a thumbnail.)
- Click a photo to see it large. Use the arrow keys to browse and Esc to close. On the right you can change the
  category or show the file in File Explorer.
- When you're done with a batch, click "Mark as Reviewed" to move it to Reviewed (it's only hidden; nothing is moved
  or deleted). "Move Back to To Review" brings it back.
- Safe to delete from your phone: after "Mark as Reviewed", Xun-Qi checks that every file in the batch is there, the
  right size and opens correctly. If everything passes, it says "Safely saved to this PC. You can delete these from
  your iPhone." with the number of photos and videos and the date range, so you can find them in Photos and delete
  them yourself. If something fails, it says "Don't delete from your phone yet" and lists the problem files. Click OK
  to dismiss (reloading the page dismisses it too). Xun-Qi never deletes files on your phone or PC.
- Search filters: below the search box you can choose a category, a type (photos / videos / documents) and a range of
  months taken. Choose filters without typing to list matching items (newest first).
- Search by image: open a photo and click "Find Similar" to list the most similar items (filters apply). No AI model
  needs to load, so it's fast. You can also drag any image from your PC into the search box (or click
  "Search by Image"); the image is only compared in memory and never saved.
- Duplicates: possible duplicates are shown side by side in groups (the original plus items that are identical or very
  similar), with resolution, file size and month taken. The best one to keep is checked for you: usually the highest
  resolution, or in a burst, a similar-resolution shot that's clearly sharper (each shows "Sharpness xx%"). Check the
  ones to keep and click "Apply": unchecked items move to the To Delete folder, and the kept ones go back to their
  category. "Not Duplicates, Keep All" moves the whole group back to their categories. In a batch, "Compare" jumps
  straight to that group.
- Move N Identical: appears on a batch when items are exactly the same as files you received before. You're asked
  to confirm first.
- To Delete: lists files moved to the To Delete folder. "Move Back" returns an item to where it was. If you're sure,
  click "Open To Delete Folder" and delete them yourself; the list updates automatically. Xun-Qi itself never deletes
  any file.
- Timeline: items grouped by year and month taken, with buttons to jump to a year. Each month shows how many items and
  the most common places; "Show All ... This Month" lists everything from that month. The filters above apply too.
- Places: reads GPS from photos and videos and looks up place names offline (nothing goes online). Taiwan shows
  "county/city + district"; other countries show "country + city". Note: place names currently come from the built-in
  data, so Taiwan places and country names are shown in Chinese. Click a place to list items taken there; the filter
  bar also has a place filter. Locations for older files are read in the background when the app starts.
  Why some items have no location: screenshots, downloaded images, or Location was turned off under Options when
  sharing from an iPhone. The folder structure doesn't change (still category\year-month).
- Export (top right): click it, then click the items to export. You can select across batches, search results and the
  timeline; "Select Page" selects everything on screen.
  "Copy to Folder..." copies to a location you choose (inside a new "Xun-Qi Export date time" folder); "Save as ZIP"
  downloads a ZIP in your browser. You can organize into folders: None, By Category, By Month or By Place. Both copy;
  your originals stay where they are and nothing is overwritten.
- Send to Phone: sends files to your phone with LocalSend (same Wi-Fi, with LocalSend open on screen).
  Open it from Export (top right) -> "Send to Phone" at the bottom. You get two choices:
  - From Library: send items you received back to your phone. Select items first, then choose this.
    Before sending, files are checked against the SHA-256 recorded when they were received; missing or changed files
    are skipped with the reason.
    Shortcuts: "Send to Phone" at the top right of each batch sends the whole batch; it's also next to a large photo.
  - From This PC: any file on your PC, using "Choose Files...", "Choose Folder..." (including subfolders) or by dragging
    files into the window. Chosen files are read where they are, not copied; dragged files are kept only until they're
    sent. These files are only sent, not added to the library.
  - Choose a phone and click "Send", then tap "Accept" on the phone. To save straight to Photos, turn on "Save to
    gallery" in LocalSend's settings on the phone. If your phone doesn't show up, click "Search Again", then
    "Scan Network". Turn off the PIN in LocalSend on the phone if it's on.
  - Files are only read, never moved or deleted. While sending you can close the window and let it continue in the
    background, or click "Cancel".
- Manage Categories (Tools): add, rename or delete categories, and edit each category's descriptions (one per line).
  Put an emoji in the icon box, or leave it empty for the built-in icon.
  Renaming moves that category's files in Sorted to a folder with the new name. Deleting a category moves its items
  to Unsorted; no files are deleted. "Documents & Receipts" and "Screenshots" have special rules: they can be renamed
  but not deleted. When you close the window you can re-sort To Review items with the new categories.
  Once you change categories in the app, the app's settings are used (saved in .xunqi-data\categories.json, so they
  survive reinstalling), and the categories in config.yaml are no longer used.
- Backup & Restore (Tools): backs up categories, review status, your corrections, custom categories, the search
  index and the phone pairing certificates, but not the photos themselves.
  Backups are saved in "files folder\Index backups"; keeping an extra copy on a USB drive or in the cloud is a good
  idea. To restore, pick a backup from the list or use "Restore from File..." to choose a backup ZIP. The current
  state is backed up automatically first, and the backup is applied when you restart the app.
- Text search (OCR): text in photos, screenshots and scanned PDFs is read. Type text like "booking code" or "1,280" in
  the search box: items with an exact text match come first, with the matching line highlighted on the thumbnail,
  followed by AI search results. Traditional and Simplified Chinese match each other, and full-width characters,
  spaces and commas don't matter ("1280" finds "1,280"). Text inside PDF, Word and TXT files is searchable the same
  way. Open a photo and use "Text" on the right to see all its text and copy it.
  An NVIDIA graphics card is used if available; otherwise the CPU (slower). When a photo has a lot of text, sorting
  leans slightly toward "Documents & Receipts".
- Video moments: videos are indexed with one frame every few seconds (up to 150 frames per video). Search compares
  every frame, the video thumbnail shows "0:42" with the best-matching frame, and opening it plays from that moment.
  If the browser can't play the format (some iPhone HEVC videos), a still from that moment is shown, and you can click
  "Open in Video Player".
- Background tasks: older photos and videos get text recognition and video indexing in the background. The top bar
  shows "Background: Text: N left · Videos: N left", with a Pause button. New files to sort always go first.
- The moon/sun button at the top right switches dark mode.
- Search with a sentence in the search box, like "sunset at the beach", "sleeping cat" or "last month's quote".

[Screenshots]
Images without camera information whose file name looks like a screenshot (Screenshot...) or whose size matches an
iPhone, iPad or Android screen go straight into "Screenshots". Photos taken with a camera are never put there.

[Safety]
- Xun-Qi only moves files it received itself, and only inside the files folder. It never reads, changes or deletes any
  other file on your PC.
- Files with the same name are renamed (photo (1).jpg) and never overwritten.
- Duplicates and similar photos only go to "Duplicates"; they move to the To Delete folder only when you click
  "Apply". Xun-Qi never deletes any file.
- The web interface can only be opened on this PC; others on your network can't reach it.

[Categories]
Food, Drinks & Desserts, Scenery, Cityscape, People, Group & Party, Pets & Animals, Plants & Flowers, Vehicles,
Documents & Receipts, Shopping, Screenshots, plus Unsorted, Possible Duplicates and Other Files.
(A files folder created in Chinese uses the Chinese category names.)
Change categories and descriptions in Tools > Manage Categories; other settings such as thresholds are in config.yaml
(open it with Notepad).

[Getting better over time]
- Items you re-categorize in the app move to the right folder and become examples, so similar photos are sorted
  correctly from then on.
- After changing categories, click Tools > Re-sort to re-sort To Review photos with the new rules (the AI doesn't run
  again, so it's fast; items you changed or already reviewed stay as they are).
- Tools > Index "Send" Items: makes photos received with "Send" searchable too (index only; nothing is moved or sorted).

[FAQ]
- Your phone doesn't see the PC: check that both are on the same Wi-Fi, the firewall allowed Xun-Qi, and the LocalSend
  desktop app is closed; pull down to refresh in LocalSend on the phone.
  If you still see only one device, set mode_select to web in config.yaml and switch modes in the web page instead.
- After changing the files folder location or the language, your phone may briefly show the old device name; it
  disappears after refreshing.
- Too many items go to Unsorted: lower min_confidence in config.yaml (for example 0.25).
- Too many or too few Suggested Review items: adjust doubt_confidence in config.yaml (lower = fewer).
- Graphics memory: the model unloads after 10 idle minutes, or use Tools > Free AI Memory.
- The files folder is on an external drive that isn't connected: Xun-Qi shows an error. Connect the drive and start
  again, or delete user_settings.json in the app folder to go back to the Desktop.
- The log is in "files folder\.xunqi-data\log.txt" (".整理器資料\log.txt" for a folder created in Chinese).
- OCR shows "No model": put the det and rec .onnx files in models\ocr\ and restart. "onnxruntime is missing": run
  setup.bat again.
- The top bar shows "Text recognition failed several times in a row and was paused": the model files are probably
  incomplete. Download them again, then click "Resume".
